## Research honesty notes

- **Scope of what was completed.** Fourteen broad Gujarat-context agents were dispatched
  first; a session-wide API/search limit terminated eleven of them mid-run. Files 01, 02 and
  03 completed. The remaining eleven topics (crime statistics, landmark cases, society,
  economy, narcotics/coastal, traffic, women & child safety, national tech stack, prohibition,
  crowd/disaster, and the original hackathon-intel agent) **were not produced.**
- **This turned out to matter far less than expected**, because the problem statements were
  then found in full — which replaced theme-guessing with primary source. Research was
  re-aimed at the actual 26 problems.
- Files 01–03 were written by agents under the standard contract (inline source URLs,
  years on statistics, `⚠️ UNVERIFIED` markers, Sources and Gaps sections). **Their
  individual claims have not been independently spot-checked by me** — treat specific
  numbers as needing verification before they go in a slide.
- Everything labelled **INFERENCE** in file 90 is my judgement, not sourced fact.
- All research here is open-source only. Nothing privileged or non-public.
