Choose the exhibit (the capture or sample) or a certificate PDF. It is hashed in this page and compared with what the package says. It is never uploaded.

What a pass does and does not show

  • Shows: the package has not been changed since it was signed; every custody entry still hashes to what is recorded; no entry has been removed from the middle or reordered; and a file you hold is, or is not, the one the package describes.
  • Does not show that the key is the laboratory's. Anyone can sign a package with their own key and it will verify. Compare the fingerprint with one you obtained by another route — a printed certificate, a phone call, the laboratory's own notice.
  • Does not show the log was never rebuilt before signing. A hash chain proves a log is consistent, not that nobody recomputed all of it. Only an external anchor could.
  • Does not show the evidence is genuine or the findings correct. It checks the record of custody, not the conclusions drawn from the file.
